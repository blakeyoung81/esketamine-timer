Privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Who this is for. Esketamine Timer is used by licensed healthcare professionals to time and document esketamine treatment sessions.

Stored on your device. Session records (the patient reference you type, dose, spray times, blood pressure and heart rate readings, and notes) are stored only on your device, protected by iOS file encryption. We cannot access them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sent to us only if you allow it. On first launch the app asks whether to share de-identified usage stats. It is off unless you choose to share. If you allow it, the app sends:

  • a random per-install identifier and a random per-session identifier
  • dose amount, device and spray counts, and timing between sprays
  • rest and observation durations and check-in responses
  • blood pressure and heart rate numbers you record
  • event names and times, app version, iOS version, and an optional clinic site code

The app never sends the patient reference you typed or any note text. The connection is write-only: the app cannot read data back from the server. You can turn sharing off at any time under ⓘ → Anonymous usage data.

What we use it for. Understanding, in aggregate, how sessions run in practice so we can improve the app.

What we don't do. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, share it with third parties, or track you across apps or websites.

Your responsibilities. Use a non-identifying reference such as initials for patients. You remain responsible for your obligations under HIPAA and applicable law.
